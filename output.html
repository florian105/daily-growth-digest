AI & Automation

Gen Z Workers Pick Human-Only Output Over AI-Assisted

Search Engine Journal • 17h ago

A Gallup survey reveals Gen Z workers trust human-only work output over AI-assisted work by more than a 2-to-1 ratio, with this trust gap continuing to widen. This preference challenges assumptions about younger generations being more AI-accepting in workplace settings.

💡 Insight: B2B leaders should reconsider AI implementation strategies, focusing on transparency and gradual integration rather than assuming younger employees will readily embrace AI-assisted workflows.

Your AI Visibility Strategy Doesn't Work Outside English

Search Engine Journal • 18h ago

Language bias in AI models creates significant visibility gaps for non-English content, forcing global brands to completely rethink their multilingual search and content strategies. Traditional SEO approaches fail when AI systems favor English-language sources.

💡 Insight: Global B2B companies must develop separate AI visibility strategies for each major market rather than translating English-first approaches, potentially requiring dedicated regional content teams.

Machine-First Architecture: AI Agents Are Here And Your Website Isn't Ready

Search Engine Journal • 19h ago

Current websites aren't built for AI agents to effectively navigate and extract information, requiring a fundamental shift to machine-first architecture. This represents a major infrastructure challenge as AI agents become primary information gatherers.

💡 Insight: B2B companies need to prioritize website restructuring for AI agent compatibility or risk becoming invisible to the next generation of automated research and procurement processes.

ChatGPT Often Retrieves But Rarely Cites Reddit Pages, Data Shows

Search Engine Journal • 15h ago

Ahrefs data reveals ChatGPT frequently retrieves information from Reddit pages but rarely provides visible citations to these sources. This hidden influence affects how AI systems present information without transparency about sources.

💡 Insight: B2B content strategists should consider Reddit as an important but invisible influence channel, potentially requiring community engagement strategies that don't expect direct attribution.

Advertising

Search Ad Growth Slows As Social & Video Gain Faster

Search Engine Journal • 19h ago

IAB's annual report shows search advertising growth has decelerated while social media and digital video advertising posted stronger year-over-year gains. This shift indicates changing consumer behavior and attention patterns across digital channels.

💡 Insight: CMOs should reallocate advertising budgets toward social and video channels while maintaining search presence, as the traditional search-dominant funnel evolves toward more visual and social discovery methods.

Should You Use Auto-Generated Creative? – Ask A PPC

Search Engine Journal • 20h ago

The role of auto-generated creative in PPC campaigns continues evolving as advertisers balance efficiency and scale benefits against maintaining creative control and regulatory compliance. This technology offers significant time savings but raises concerns about brand consistency.

💡 Insight: B2B marketers should implement hybrid approaches using auto-generated creative for scale while maintaining human oversight for brand-critical campaigns and regulated industries.

B2B SaaS

VC in 2026: 75% of All the Money Is Going to Just 5 VC Funds. And To Just 5 "Startups."

SaaStr • 20h ago

PitchBook-NVCA data shows extreme concentration in venture capital, with 73.1% of Q1 2026 LP capital going to just five VC firms, while the next ten firms received only 15.4%. This consolidation reflects risk-averse investing focused on proven players.

💡 Insight: B2B startups outside the elite tier must develop alternative funding strategies and demonstrate exceptional traction earlier, as traditional VC paths become increasingly narrow and competitive.
