AI Gross Margins Are Up, R&D Spend Is Way Up, AI Pricing is a Mess: 5 Key Takeaways from ICONIQ's State of AI Report

SaaStr • 17h ago

ICONIQ Capital's latest State of AI report surveyed 300 software executives including CEOs, CROs, and CFOs across companies ranging from sub-$5M to $1B+ ARR. The report reveals key trends in AI product development, showing improvements in gross margins alongside significant increases in R&D spending. However, AI pricing strategies remain challenging for most companies to optimize effectively.

💡 Insight: B2B leaders should prepare for increased R&D investments while developing clearer AI pricing strategies, as margin improvements may be offset by development costs in the near term.

How Nebius Built a $4B+ GPU Cloud in 18 Months: The 7 Top Lessons Learned

SaaStr • 20h ago

Nebius spun out of Yandex after 2022 geopolitical disruptions and built a GPU cloud business valued at over $4 billion in just 18 months. Starting essentially from scratch with their brand, they rapidly scaled from zero US presence to becoming a major hyperscaler. The company's success offers valuable lessons for B2B startups on rapid scaling and market entry strategies.

💡 Insight: The rapid success demonstrates how geopolitical disruptions can create market opportunities for agile B2B companies willing to rebuild and pivot quickly with focused execution.
