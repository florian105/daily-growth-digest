Growth Marketing

The phrase 'marketing sourced revenue' is a fiction that needs to be eradicated

Marketing Week • 0h ago

The article argues that 'marketing sourced revenue' as a B2B success metric is fundamentally flawed and counterproductive. This backwards-looking attribution method creates unnecessary friction between sales and marketing teams while potentially undermining marketing's credibility. The concept fails to accurately capture marketing's true contribution to revenue generation.

💡 Insight: B2B leaders should reconsider their attribution models and focus on collaborative metrics that unite sales and marketing rather than create departmental silos.

Anthropic Just Hit $14 Billion in ARR. Up From $1 Billion Just 14 Months Ago.

SaaStr • 18h ago

Anthropic has achieved remarkable growth, reaching $14 billion in ARR from just $1 billion 14 months prior, representing a 1,300% growth rate. The company simultaneously closed a massive $30 billion Series G funding round at a $380 billion valuation, making it the second-largest private tech round ever. This valuation represents more than double their previous $183 billion valuation from just five months earlier.

💡 Insight: The explosive growth demonstrates the potential scale of AI market opportunity, but also raises questions about sustainable growth rates and market saturation for B2B leaders considering AI strategies.
