We Had a Bug in One of Our 20+ AI Agents. It Took Forever to Figure Out Which One. This is Going to Be a Real Problem.

SaaStr • 16h ago

SaaStr experienced a debugging nightmare when one of their 20+ AI agents was providing outdated information about an event. The real challenge wasn't fixing the bug, but identifying which specific agent among their extensive AI infrastructure was responsible for the error.

💡 Insight: As B2B companies scale AI implementations, establishing robust monitoring and debugging systems becomes critical infrastructure - operational complexity grows exponentially with each additional AI agent deployed.
