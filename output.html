Disrupting 'zombie-like behaviour': How brands can stand out in commodified categories

Marketing Week • 1h ago

Brands operating in commodity categories face significant challenges in differentiation and must actively work against established category norms to drive growth. The article explores strategies for breaking through 'zombie-like behavior' where companies follow predictable patterns that fail to capture customer attention or create meaningful differentiation.

💡 Insight: B2B leaders should audit their positioning strategy to identify areas where they may be following industry conventions rather than creating distinctive value propositions that resonate with target customers.

How To Get The Perfect Budget Mix For SEO And PPC via @sejournal, @brookeosmundson

Search Engine Journal • 10h ago

The article provides guidance on optimizing budget allocation between SEO and PPC channels for maximum ROI. It emphasizes the importance of clear performance modeling, transparent forecasting, and understanding how each channel contributes to pipeline development, customer acquisition costs, and strategic growth objectives.

💡 Insight: CMOs should implement integrated measurement frameworks that track both channels' contribution to pipeline velocity and customer lifetime value, not just individual channel metrics.

Google Announces AI Mode Checkout Protocol, Business Agent via @sejournal, @MattGSouthern

Search Engine Journal • 16h ago

Google has introduced new AI-powered features including checkout capabilities in AI Mode and a Business Agent feature that enables branded AI chat directly in Search results. These features are currently available to U.S. retailers and represent a significant shift in how customers can interact with businesses through search.

💡 Insight: B2B companies should evaluate how AI-powered search interactions might affect their customer acquisition funnel and consider preparing for similar B2B-focused AI search features.
