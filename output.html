Who's Left In the 10x ARR Club? The Incredible Shrinking Elite of Public B2B Companies Still Trading at 10x+ Revenue. But … The Best Is Yet To Come.

SaaStr • 16h ago

The public B2B SaaS valuation landscape has dramatically shifted from 2020's peak when 60% of companies traded at 10x+ revenue multiples. Today, the elite 10x ARR club has shrunk significantly, with companies like Asana falling from 89x to 3x ARR. The median SaaS multiple has dropped from over 18x to much lower levels, representing a fundamental reset in market expectations.

💡 Insight: CEOs and boards must recalibrate growth expectations and fundraising strategies, as the era of premium SaaS valuations has ended and operational efficiency now trumps growth-at-all-costs.
