Growth Marketing

The Content Moat Is Dead. The Context Moat Is What Survives

Search Engine Journal • 18h ago

Traditional content marketing strategies are becoming ineffective as AI makes well-written guides commoditized and easily replicable. The article argues that businesses must now focus on creating 'context moats' - unique, irreplaceable contextual information that provides genuine value and cannot be easily replicated by AI systems.

💡 Insight: B2B companies must pivot their content strategies from generic educational content to proprietary insights, case studies, and contextual expertise that only they can provide to maintain competitive advantage in an AI-driven landscape.
