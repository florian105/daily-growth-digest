The Cowan Paradox: Why AI Agents Won't Let You Do Less Work. They'll Make You Do More.

SaaStr • 16h ago

AI agents are creating a paradox where instead of reducing workload, they're accelerating business expectations and competitive pressure. Companies must now deliver faster responses, more frequent releases, and higher service levels to match AI-enabled competitors. This challenges the common narrative that AI will simply automate work away.

💡 Insight: B2B leaders need to reframe AI adoption from cost reduction to competitive acceleration - investing in AI capabilities not to do less, but to meet rapidly escalating market expectations.
