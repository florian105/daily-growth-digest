Growth Marketing

Most marketers feel overwhelmed, undervalued and emotionally exhausted

Marketing Week • 0h ago

Marketing Week data reveals that most marketers are experiencing significant mental health challenges, feeling overwhelmed, undervalued, and emotionally exhausted. Despite these concerning levels of burnout, two-fifths of marketers would not communicate these feelings to their managers.

💡 Insight: This signals a critical talent retention and performance risk for B2B organizations, suggesting the need for proactive employee wellness programs and better manager training to identify and address marketing team burnout.

I Wrote Off $4M to $0. My Co-Investor Marked Up the Same Deal to $30M. Here's What Founders Need to Know About VC Incentives.

SaaStr • 16h ago

A SaaStr Fund investor wrote off $4 million in a portfolio company to zero, while their co-investor marked up the same investment from $15 million to $30 million. This stark contrast in valuation approaches highlights the different incentive structures and evaluation methods among VCs for identical investments.

💡 Insight: B2B executives should understand that VC valuations can vary dramatically based on fund incentives and strategies, impacting fundraising expectations and investor selection decisions.
