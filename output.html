Dear SaaStr: Can I Convert My Customer Success Managers into Forward Deployed Engineers?

SaaStr • 21h ago

Converting Customer Success Managers to Forward Deployed Engineers is only viable in specific cases and typically requires hiring different skill sets. CSMs focus on relationship management, renewal risk mitigation, and expansion across 8-12 accounts, while FDEs require deep technical implementation skills. The roles demand fundamentally different competencies and mindsets.
