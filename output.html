Dear SaaStr: Should I Hire More FDEs or CSMs for Our AI Agent Product?

SaaStr • 19h ago

For AI B2B products, deployment has become the primary constraint rather than retention. The decision between hiring Field Deployment Engineers (FDEs) or Customer Success Managers (CSMs) depends on identifying the actual bottleneck. If customers sign but don't go live or underperform after deployment, FDEs are needed over CSMs.

💡 Insight: B2B SaaS leaders should prioritize deployment expertise over traditional customer success roles when scaling AI products, as successful implementation drives retention more than relationship management.
