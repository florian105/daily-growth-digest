PE Is No Longer Coming to the Rescue. And That Probably Means No One Will.

SaaStr • 16h ago

Despite M&A hitting record highs, private equity has largely abandoned typical B2B/SaaS companies in the AI age. The benefits of increased deal activity are concentrated among AI-focused companies, leaving traditional SaaS businesses without their historical safety net of PE acquisition.

💡 Insight: B2B CEOs must prioritize sustainable growth and profitability over growth-at-all-costs strategies, as PE bailouts are no longer available for underperforming companies.

SaaStr • 17h ago

Zylo's SaaS Management Index reveals that B2B software spending increased 8% while application counts remained flat, indicating companies are spending more on existing tools rather than adding new ones. The standout trend is AI spending surge of 108%, showing where budget priorities are shifting.

💡 Insight: CROs should audit their current software stack for optimization opportunities while strategically investing in AI tools that can demonstrably improve sales productivity and customer outcomes.
