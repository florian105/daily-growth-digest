B2B SaaS

Your Critical Product Goal for 2026: Your Customers Truly, Honestly See Your AI Agent As Part of the Team. For Real.

SaaStr • 16h ago

B2B companies must prioritize creating AI agents that customers can interact with as naturally as they do with ChatGPT or Claude. The goal is to enable customers to simply tell the app what they want and have it execute tasks almost magically, making the AI feel like a genuine team member rather than just a tool.

💡 Insight: B2B product leaders should focus on conversational AI interfaces that match consumer AI expectations, as this could become a key differentiator in customer retention and satisfaction.

The Latest Carta Data: VC Deals Are Up Only 3%, But $$ Are Up 130%

SaaStr • 18h ago

New Carta data reveals a significant concentration of venture capital, with deal volume up only 3% while total dollars invested surged 130%. This indicates massive capital concentration in fewer, larger deals, primarily driven by AI investments over the past 18+ months.

Starbucks claims 'broad' appeal driving 'customer connection'

Marketing Week • 15h ago

Starbucks CEO Brian Niccol emphasizes that focusing solely on loyalty members at the expense of infrequent customers is unhealthy for business. The company is pursuing a broad appeal strategy to maintain customer connections across all user segments, not just high-frequency customers.

💡 Insight: B2B companies should avoid over-indexing on their most engaged customers and ensure their go-to-market strategy appeals to occasional users who may represent significant growth potential.
