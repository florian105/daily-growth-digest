ChatGPT Now Crawls 3.6x More Than Googlebot: What 24M Requests Reveal

Search Engine Journal • 3h ago

OpenAI's ChatGPT crawler has surpassed Google's dominance in web crawling, making 3.6x more requests than Googlebot across 24 million total requests. This shift represents a fundamental change in how content is discovered and indexed on the web, with AI search engines becoming increasingly aggressive in content acquisition.

💡 Insight: B2B companies need to optimize their content strategy for AI crawlers, not just traditional search engines, as AI-powered discovery becomes the new battleground for visibility.

Trust In AI Search Could Drop With Ads, Survey Shows

Search Engine Journal • 12h ago

An Ipsos survey reveals that 63% of U.S. adults believe advertising in AI search results would reduce their trust in the technology. This consumer sentiment poses challenges for the monetization of AI search platforms and could impact how businesses approach AI-powered advertising strategies.

💡 Insight: CMOs should prepare for potential consumer resistance to AI search advertising and consider how trust factors will influence their digital marketing mix as AI search adoption grows.

ChatGPT Search Is Citing Fewer Sites, Data Shows

Search Engine Journal • 18h ago

ChatGPT Search has reduced the number of websites it cites per response following the implementation of GPT-5.3 Instant as the default experience. This consolidation means fewer sources are being referenced in AI search results, potentially concentrating traffic to a smaller pool of authoritative sites.

💡 Insight: B2B content marketers face increased competition for AI search visibility, making domain authority and content quality more critical than ever for maintaining traffic flow.

The Top 6 Search Engines Market Share & The AI Search Engines To Watch

Search Engine Journal • 19h ago

Google's search market share is declining while AI-powered search engines gain traction, signaling a fundamental shift in where discovery happens online. OpenAI's $122B "VC Round" Is Vendor Deals, Contingent Capital, and a Guaranteed Return It Arguably Can't Afford
