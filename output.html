The Honest Reason AI Will Beat Many Sales Reps: They Don't Really Do A Lot Of The Work. But Agents Do and Will.

SaaStr • 17h ago

AI threatens to replace underperforming sales representatives who aren't fully engaged in their roles, rather than high-performing sales professionals. The article argues that AI agents are becoming capable of handling tasks that many sales reps currently neglect or perform inadequately. The focus should be on identifying and retaining sales talent that truly adds value beyond what AI can accomplish.

💡 Insight: Sales leaders need to evaluate their teams not just on results, but on work ethic and engagement levels, as AI will increasingly expose and replace low-effort performers while augmenting top performers.
