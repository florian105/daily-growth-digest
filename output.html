SaaS Isn't Dead. But the Way You Used to Win in B2B? That's Gone.

SaaStr • 16h ago

Despite massive SaaS sell-offs since mid-2025 and compressed multiples, B2B software isn't actually dying. Enterprise software spending is accelerating in 2026 at record rates, indicating continued demand for business tools. However, traditional go-to-market strategies and competitive approaches that previously drove success in B2B markets are no longer effective.

💡 Insight: B2B leaders must fundamentally reimagine their growth strategies and market approach, as legacy tactics that drove past success are becoming obsolete in the current market environment.

Canva Crosses a Stunning $4B ARR, Growing 35% (!). But What Would It Be Worth Today?

SaaStr • 18h ago

Canva has achieved $4 billion in ARR while maintaining impressive 35% growth, representing one of the most extraordinary B2B growth stories ever. The company scaled from $23 million in 2018 to $4 billion in 2025, achieving 173x growth over seven years. Despite starting as a consumer-focused product, Canva has successfully transitioned into a major B2B player but receives limited recognition for this achievement.

💡 Insight: The boundary between B2C and B2B products is increasingly blurred, suggesting that companies with strong consumer adoption can successfully scale into enterprise markets with the right product evolution and go-to-market strategy.
