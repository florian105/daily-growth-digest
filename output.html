AI Search Is Eating Itself & The SEO Industry Is The Source

Search Engine Journal • 19h ago

AI search systems are trapped in a self-reinforcing loop where synthetic content generated by AI is being fed back into retrieval systems and presented as factual information. The SEO industry's increasing reliance on AI-generated content is contributing to this degradation of information quality.

💡 Insight: B2B content strategies must prioritize authentic, human-verified content to maintain credibility and avoid being devalued by AI search systems that can detect synthetic content patterns.
