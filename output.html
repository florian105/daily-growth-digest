Search Engine Journal • 18h ago

Forward-thinking organizations are integrating SEO considerations earlier in their development process by establishing formal commissioning workflows. 💡 Insight: Organizations that embed SEO requirements upstream in their product development cycle will achieve better organic visibility and reduce costly post-launch optimization efforts.

SEO Test Shows It's Trivial To Rank Misinformation On Google

Search Engine Journal • 22h ago

A controlled SEO experiment revealed how easily false information can achieve high rankings in Google Search results and subsequently spread across other websites. This demonstrates significant vulnerabilities in search algorithms that competitors or bad actors could exploit.
