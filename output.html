Search Referral Traffic Down 60% For Small Publishers, Data Shows via @sejournal, @MattGSouthern

Search Engine Journal • 7h ago

Chartbeat data reveals a dramatic decline in search referral traffic for small publishers, falling 60% over two years compared to just 22% for large publishers. This disparity highlights how Google's algorithm changes and market dynamics disproportionately impact smaller content creators.

💡 Insight: B2B companies relying on content marketing should diversify traffic sources and consider partnerships with larger publishers to maintain visibility.

Google AI Overviews Cut Germany's Top Organic CTR By 59% via @sejournal, @MattGSouthern

Search Engine Journal • 8h ago

SISTRIX's analysis of over 100 million German keywords shows AI Overviews dramatically reduced click-through rates for top organic search results from 27% to 11%. The impact varies significantly by industry, indicating some sectors are more vulnerable to AI-driven search changes.

💡 Insight: B2B companies must prepare for reduced organic search traffic and adapt SEO strategies to compete with AI-generated summaries.

SaaStr • 17h ago

Analysis shows consistent year-over-year growth rate declines across major public software companies since 2021, with Salesforce dropping from 25% to 8% growth and Snowflake from 106% to 24%.
