Medallia Is Just the Opening Act: 12+ PE SaaS Deals With $50B+ in Debt at Risk of Blowing Up

SaaStr • 17h ago

Private equity firm Thoma Bravo lost control of Medallia to lenders after a failed $6.4 billion acquisition in 2021, wiping out $5.1 billion in equity.
