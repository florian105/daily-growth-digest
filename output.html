The Science Of How AI Picks Its Sources

Search Engine Journal • 18h ago

New research analyzing ChatGPT citation patterns reveals that AI systems heavily favor a small concentration of high-authority domains when selecting sources. The study found that broad, cluster-based content pages significantly outperform single-intent content in AI visibility, suggesting a shift in content strategy requirements for organic discovery through AI platforms.

💡 Insight: B2B companies need to rethink content strategy to capture AI-driven search traffic - comprehensive, multi-topic pages may be more valuable than traditional single-focus SEO content for reaching prospects through AI assistants.

Research Shows Where Persona Prompting Works And When It Backfires

Search Engine Journal • 19h ago

Academic research demonstrates that persona prompts (like 'you are an expert') can significantly damage factual accuracy in AI responses for certain task types, while proving beneficial for others. The findings challenge common AI prompting practices and suggest organizations need more nuanced approaches when deploying AI tools for different business functions.

💡 Insight: Companies using AI for content creation, analysis, or customer interactions should audit their prompting strategies - persona prompts may be undermining accuracy in critical business applications.
