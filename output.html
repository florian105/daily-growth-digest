Growth Marketing

AI Overview CTR Fell 61%, But Clicks Didn't Collapse

Search Engine Journal • 3h ago

Seer Interactive research shows that brand-cited AI Overview click-through rates dropped 61% as impressions grew faster than actual clicks across cited pages. Despite the significant CTR decline, the overall click volume didn't experience a complete collapse, suggesting the impact may be less severe than the percentage suggests.

💡 Insight: B2B marketing leaders need to reassess their SEO and content strategies to account for AI Overview impact on organic traffic, while recognizing that quality content may still drive meaningful engagement despite lower CTRs.

Google Pushes 'Bounce Clicks' Explanation For AI Overview Traffic Loss

Search Engine Journal • 19h ago

Google's Liz Reid claims AI Overviews primarily reduce 'bounce clicks' rather than deeper, more valuable website visits. However, Google has not provided concrete data to support this assertion, leaving businesses uncertain about the true impact on their traffic quality and conversion rates.

💡 Insight: Marketing and revenue leaders should closely monitor their own analytics to validate Google's claims, as the distinction between bounce clicks and quality traffic directly impacts lead generation and conversion metrics.

Google's Updates Push Search Further Into Task Completion

Search Engine Journal • 20h ago

Google's latest search updates are increasingly focused on helping users complete tasks directly within search results rather than directing them to websites. This shift challenges traditional reporting mechanisms that businesses rely on to track customer acquisition and engagement through organic search channels.

💡 Insight: B2B leaders must adapt their marketing attribution models and invest in new analytics approaches to capture customer interactions that increasingly happen outside their owned digital properties.

B2B SaaS

If You Are at Scale, You Now Have 2 Full-Time Jobs: Keep Your Installed Base Happy. And Winning the AI Agent War in Your Space.

SaaStr • 18h ago

B2B CEOs at companies over $50M ARR are facing a dual challenge: maintaining their existing customer base while simultaneously competing in the emerging AI agent market within their industry vertical. Most leaders are focusing primarily on their traditional business while underinvesting in the AI transformation that could determine their long-term competitive position.

💡 Insight: Successful B2B executives must now allocate significant resources and attention to AI agent development while maintaining operational excellence, requiring new organizational structures and strategic planning approaches.

A Terrible, Terrible Week in Software Stocks. But Not Necessarily a Terrible 12 Months.

SaaStr • 20h ago

Major B2B software stocks experienced significant declines with ServiceNow down 15-17%, IBM off 10%, Salesforce down 7%, Oracle down 5%, and Intuit down 7%. The selloff was triggered by earnings from two major legacy software companies, despite both beating expectations. However, the short-term market reaction may not reflect longer-term business fundamentals.

💡 Insight: B2B leaders should view current market volatility as a potential opportunity for strategic investments, acquisitions, or talent acquisition while maintaining focus on fundamental business metrics rather than short-term stock performance.
