B2B SaaS

The 2x ARR Club: Don't Let It Be You

SaaStr • 16h ago

A growing number of public B2B companies are trading at dangerously low valuations below 2.5x revenue, with many below 1x. This trend has accelerated since the SaaS Crash of 2026, creating a new category of undervalued companies that face significant operational and strategic challenges.

💡 Insight: B2B leaders must focus on fundamental business metrics and operational efficiency to avoid joining this low-valuation cohort, as market conditions continue to punish companies with weak unit economics or growth profiles.

Salesforce Now Has 3+ Pricing Models for Agentforce. And Maybe Right Now, That's The Way to Do It.

SaaStr • 18h ago

Salesforce has launched three different pricing models for Agentforce within 18 months: $2 per conversation, $0.10 per action via Flex Credits, and $125/user/month for per-user licenses. This multi-model approach reflects the challenge of pricing AI products in an evolving market.

💡 Insight: B2B companies introducing AI products should consider flexible pricing strategies that accommodate different customer use cases and market maturity levels, rather than committing to a single pricing model early.

Growth Marketing

'More impact, less waste': Coca-Cola CFO praises impact of marketing 'transformation'

Marketing Week • 14h ago

Coca-Cola has transformed its marketing approach to be more effective and efficient for the modern landscape. The company's CFO highlights how this reinvention has delivered measurable improvements in both marketing effectiveness and operational efficiency.

💡 Insight: Large enterprise transformations in marketing operations can deliver significant ROI when focused on eliminating waste while maintaining impact, providing a blueprint for B2B marketing optimization.

'A sprawling ecosystem': How have marketers' tech stacks got so out of hand?

Marketing Week • 18h ago

Marketing technology stacks have become unwieldy due to outdated systems and lack of ownership clarity. Marketers need to streamline their technology investments to maximize value and efficiency rather than continuing to add tools without strategic integration.

💡 Insight: CMOs should audit and consolidate their martech stacks to reduce complexity, improve ROI, and ensure better integration across marketing operations - focusing on fewer, more strategic tools rather than expanding vendor relationships.

5 Ways Emerging Businesses Can Show up in ChatGPT, Gemini & Perplexity

Search Engine Journal • 1h ago

AI models typically favor established brands over smaller competitors when providing recommendations. The article outlines specific strategies emerging businesses can use to increase their visibility and mentions across major AI platforms like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity.

💡 Insight: B2B companies, especially emerging ones, need to develop AI-first content and SEO strategies to ensure their solutions are surfaced by AI tools that prospects increasingly use for vendor discovery and evaluation.

The Science Of How AI Pays Attention

Search Engine Journal • 17h ago

Analysis of 1.2 million ChatGPT citations reveals that AI systems prefer content that is front-loaded with key information, entity-rich, and provides definitive answers. Traditional long-form 'ultimate guide' formats are less likely to be cited by AI systems.

💡 Insight: B2B content strategies must evolve to prioritize concise, authoritative, and entity-rich formats that align with how AI systems process and cite information, moving away from traditional long-form SEO content.

Google Text Ad Click Share Rises Sharply In Some Verticals

Search Engine Journal • 16h ago

Analysis of approximately 16,000 U.S. search queries shows text ads gaining significant click share, increasing by 7-13 percentage points year-over-year in certain verticals. This suggests a shift in user behavior and potentially changes in Google's search result layouts.

💡 Insight: B2B companies should reassess their paid search strategies as text ads show renewed effectiveness, potentially requiring increased investment in Google Ads to capture growing click share opportunities.

ISBA launches guidance on how brands can approach proprietary media

Marketing Week • 0h ago

ISBA has released guidance to help brands navigate proprietary media, where agencies purchase media inventory and resell it at higher rates to clients. The guidance focuses on transparency, governance, and effectiveness considerations for brands using this media buying approach.

💡 Insight: B2B marketing leaders should review their media agency relationships and contracts to ensure transparency in proprietary media arrangements, potentially impacting media buying costs and performance measurement.
