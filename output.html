What Google & Microsoft Earnings Say About Search via @sejournal, @MattGSouthern

Search Engine Journal • 20h ago

Recent earnings from Alphabet and Microsoft reveal contrasting trends in search advertising revenue. While core search ad revenue continues to grow for both tech giants, Google's Network revenue has declined to $6.97 billion, marking another quarter of downward trajectory.

💡 Insight: The decline in Google Network revenue signals potential shifts in digital advertising landscapes that B2B companies should monitor for their paid acquisition strategies.

SaaStr • 18h ago

B2B CEOs are increasingly focused on making their products more agentic but may be overcomplicating the approach. Rather than hiring Chief AI Officers or creating specialized teams, the most practical first step is ensuring existing APIs are optimized for AI agent interactions, which most companies have overlooked.
