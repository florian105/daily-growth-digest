Growth Marketing

Haleon CMO departs as it appoints first chief growth officer

Marketing Week • 0h ago

Haleon's CMO Tamara Rogers is departing after leading marketing through the company's transition from GSK spinoff to standalone entity. The company is simultaneously appointing its first chief growth officer role.

💡 Insight: The trend toward Chief Growth Officer roles signals a shift from traditional marketing leadership to growth-focused executives who bridge marketing, sales, and revenue operations.

Culture of silence: Why marketers aren't talking about their mental health

Marketing Week • 1h ago

Marketing professionals are experiencing mental health challenges but aren't seeking help due to fragile job security, limited trust, and fear of judgment within their organizations. This silence is creating a broader cultural issue in marketing departments.

💡 Insight: Mental health issues in marketing teams can significantly impact performance and retention - leaders need to create psychologically safe environments to maintain team effectiveness.

'Single-minded on growth': How Yotel is 'connecting the dots' between brand, culture and growth

Marketing Week • 18h ago

Tech-hotel brand Yotel is restructuring its marketing leadership to integrate brand strategy with guest experience, company culture, and commercial growth as it scales from 23 hotels to 100 by 2031. The approach puts brand at the center of all growth initiatives.

💡 Insight: Successful scaling requires integrating brand strategy across all customer touchpoints and internal culture, not treating marketing as a separate function.

OpenAI Begins Testing Ads In ChatGPT For Free And Go Users

Search Engine Journal • 10h ago

OpenAI is testing advertisements in ChatGPT for free and Go tier users in the U.S. The ads appear below responses and are clearly labeled as sponsored content, while paid tiers remain ad-free.

💡 Insight: ChatGPT's move into advertising creates new B2B marketing opportunities and signals the maturation of AI platforms as advertising channels.

Why Your SEO KPIs Are Failing Your Business (And How To Fix Them)

Search Engine Journal • 18h ago

Many businesses are tracking SEO metrics that don't align with actual business growth and revenue outcomes. The article advocates for moving beyond vanity metrics to KPIs that reflect modern customer acquisition and growth patterns.

💡 Insight: B2B leaders need to audit their digital marketing KPIs to ensure they're measuring business impact rather than activity metrics that don't correlate with revenue.

PPC Budget Rebalancing: How AI Changes Where Marketing Budgets Are Spent

Search Engine Journal • 19h ago

AI is fundamentally changing PPC budget allocation by shifting focus from traditional channel-based planning to conversion probability-based spending. This approach optimizes budget distribution based on likelihood of conversion rather than historical channel performance.

💡 Insight: CMOs should consider AI-driven budget allocation strategies that prioritize conversion probability over traditional channel silos to maximize ROI.

Databricks at $5.4B, Growing 65%. Is It the Most Unstoppable Company in B2B Right Now?

SaaStr • 14h ago

While many B2B companies are experiencing slowing growth rates, Databricks announced $5.4 billion in revenue with 65% growth. This performance stands in stark contrast to other major players like Snowflake growing at 25-30% and most public software companies struggling to reach 40% growth.

💡 Insight: Databricks' exceptional growth in a slowing market demonstrates the power of AI-native platforms and suggests significant market share capture opportunities for well-positioned B2B companies.

Inference is the New Sales & Marketing Spend

SaaStr • 16h ago

AI companies are reframing high inference costs not as margin problems but as customer acquisition cost replacements. When AI products become so viral and competitive through superior performance, they can reduce traditional sales and marketing expenses.

💡 Insight: B2B leaders should evaluate whether investing in product intelligence and AI capabilities could reduce traditional CAC while improving customer experience and retention.

Google: Actually, AI Hasn't Hurt Search at All. In Fact, It's Accelerating It.

SaaStr • 18h ago

Despite predictions that ChatGPT and AI would kill Google Search, Google reported Q4 2025 Search revenue of $63.1 billion with 17% YoY growth - the fastest growth rate in recent periods. This challenges the narrative that AI tools are displacing traditional search.

💡 Insight: The resilience of Google Search suggests that established platforms can successfully integrate AI rather than be displaced by it, offering lessons for B2B companies facing AI disruption.
