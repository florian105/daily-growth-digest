The #1 Conceit in B2B at Scale: Masking a Slowdown in Net New Customers

SaaStr • 16h ago

B2B companies at scale often mask declining customer acquisition rates, which becomes a fatal flaw that kills more businesses than product issues or market timing. This concealment of slowing net new customer growth represents the most dangerous self-deception in B2B operations. The pattern has been observed across hundreds of B2B companies and consistently leads to business failure.

💡 Insight: CEOs and CROs must prioritize transparent customer acquisition metrics and address declining new customer growth immediately rather than masking it with other metrics, as this transparency is critical for long-term survival.

The Trade Desk Was Priced for Perfection. Then Growth Slowed. Now It's the Worst Performing Stock in the S&P 500.

SaaStr • 19h ago

The Trade Desk, once the darling of adtech and a profitable founder-led company with consistent epic growth rates, has become the worst performing stock in the S&P 500 over the past 12 months. Despite building the leading independent demand-side platform and positioning itself as the Switzerland of programmatic advertising, the company's growth slowdown led to dramatic stock underperformance.

💡 Insight: High-growth B2B companies trading at premium valuations face severe market punishment when growth rates decelerate, highlighting the critical importance of managing investor expectations and maintaining sustainable growth trajectories.
